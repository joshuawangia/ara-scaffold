# ARA from premises to papers: construction, search, and the limits of licensed inference

*An anonymised methodology report · Version 5, concept-based revision · correspondence through 30 October 2026, 06:46:47 UTC · live checkpoints explicitly dated*

## Abstract

ARA separates a bank of licensed moves (L), a frozen theory assembled from source commitments (T), and proposed hypotheses or targets (H). The scaffold was **designed and directed by a human, and built largely by AI coding agents under that direction**. This report organizes its development and use around those boundaries. Construction chooses which literature can enter T; window failures, source stance and atomic formalization can prevent a useful inference even when a passage has been acquired. Forks preserve one freeze while varying target context or hypotheses. Bank checks and polarity search then distinguish an admissible step from an unclosed obligation. The central representation bottleneck was measured directly in the shared freeze: 141/160 commitments were paper-prefixed atoms, and only two of 147 symbols spanned papers through a single alignment bridge. With no shared vocabulary in the source commitments themselves, wider bank-licensed search mostly constructed Boolean packages rather than the intended cross-paper inference. Completed packed, unpacked and externally seeded tests produced 100 OPEN routes and no substantive CLOSED-derived contribution. ARA did propose near-T2 targets; the exact T2 formulation came from a separate literature dossier. Output gates exposed unstable audits, citation loss and unsupported prose. The available fallback PDF analyzes those outcomes; two native sign-argument drafts were refused. Gap2 stopped before freeze. Gap3, shared-catalogue/wider-alignment repair, disclosed hypothesis-premise support and proposal-mode writing remain pending; the coordinator requested a stop to the larger forward run after its truncated census. The latter approaches separate arguing for a new bridge principle from mechanically deriving a conditional result. Deterministic figures, recorded human decisions, source hashes, worker credits and scoped cost accounting make the evidence and its limits inspectable.

## 1. L, T and H: the architecture and responsibility boundary

The human chose the research area, supplied the bibliography, directed the scaffold, approved budgets and provider changes, and set acceptance and submission priorities. Coding agents implemented, reviewed and operated it. ARA generation used separate provider calls; isolation of those calls does not establish isolation of development workers. The operating rule prohibited the human from supplying original philosophical arguments, premises, distinctions or a personal view. Recording actions does not independently certify competition compliance. The human-action ledger now contains **38 timestamped entries**, plus earlier untimed design and bibliography preparation. Worker use of a native `HUMAN` input boundary does not count as a distinct human intervention. [R1–R3, R34]

Every later effort is an instance of the same process: represent admissible evidence in T; propose something in H; try to license a move through L; record what remains open; and gate any written account on its actual support. Proposal-mode writing and proposed hypothesis premises extend this process in different ways. Neither licenses quietly converting a new idea into source evidence.

ARA separates **L**, the versioned bank of permitted move forms; **T**, the working table of source-linked commitments; and **H**, model-proposed cuts or hypotheses. A model is not allowed to create a license by asserting that a step is plausible. A step must instantiate an existing bank entry. Deductive entries require both shape checks and Z3 entailment. Defeasible entries require the relevant scheme and its critical questions; a failed or unsupported critical question leaves an obligation open. [R3]

Rich extraction records are immutable source evidence. A construction lifecycle selects a bounded working subset, formalizes it, records source stance and omitted support, and then freezes T. Search uses that freeze. A verified fork copies the frozen prefix and provenance, proposes new targets, and uses a separate cache. It reuses premises, not statistical independence. Reported or attacked source positions are not silently converted into endorsed premises. [R3, R4]

{{fig:architecture}}

For each target, ARA searches both the target and its negation. The engine records proposed, licensed, discarded, and unresolved moves. A closed route means the engine’s formal obligations were met under that version’s policy. It does not establish that a source was faithfully translated, its premises are true, the result is new to the literature, or its prose is good philosophy.

Originality was pursued through several independent checks: avoiding direct restatement of T, testing single-support entailment and tautologies, classifying whether the target states an own thesis, checking the relation between its text and formal definition, requiring distinct source supports, and later comparing with retrieved literature. The gate design changed substantially. Its defects are part of the method’s history, not evidence of an invariant guarantee. [R9–R11]

### 4.1 Formal search and the outcome census

The operator-approved acceptable pilot required a clean native selection boundary, source coverage of at least **20 of 26 papers**, at least **three** genuinely derived routes, and readable summaries/reporting. Each qualifying route had to state a novel own thesis, avoid single-commitment entailment, use at least **two distinct supports from two papers**, respect stance, and contain a licensed inference. ### 4.2 Novelty gates must allow synthesis without allowing padding

The whole-T novelty gate was another substantive design error. Requiring that the union of T not entail a target prevents deductive synthesis across papers, even when no single paper entails it. The corrected local policy allows union entailment and tests each paper’s endorsed closure separately, while retaining anti-padding, stance, fidelity, and support checks. The placeholder regression combines P from one paper with P → Q from another and derives Q. This demonstrates the corrected mechanism, not a real literature result. The final bounded real-freeze census, at depth **two** and a **500-consequence cap**, found **zero qualifying candidates**. An earlier six-candidate census was superseded after a packaging defect was fixed. Cross-paper symbol alignment was still a separate integration boundary. [R11]

The R28 repairs distinguish a genuinely joint consequence from Boolean packaging and reject inconsistent endorsed-source unions rather than deriving arbitrary novelty from contradiction. Kappa4's later xi29 review closed R28-F1/F2 at the integrated head. Gap1r's previously observed packaging leads were rejected under that repair; its bounded formula union had been SAT, so the inconsistent-union trigger was absent in that freeze. A passing gate is still weaker than literature novelty or faithful source interpretation. [R18, R27]

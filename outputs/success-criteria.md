# ARA entry: success criteria (operator-approved 2026-10-27)

## Acceptable pilot (all required)
1. **Clean run.** It reaches native `awaiting_selection` with complete receipts: every model call and
   every human action logged. No Claude-subscription calls and no API spend.
2. **Coverage.** Frozen T draws on **at least 20 of the 26 papers**. Every commitment has a page citation
   and a stance label (endorsed, reported or attacked).
3. **Real routes.** **At least 3 routes are CLOSED-derived.** Each one:
   - targets an own-thesis, novel claim;
   - is not entailed by any single commitment;
   - uses at least 2 distinct supports from at least 2 different papers;
   - uses every support in line with its stance;
   - contains at least one licensed inference.
4. **Readable output.** A model-written summary for every route, and a report that renders.

## Successful entry
1. An essay from the operator-chosen route, between 4,500 and 6,000 words.
2. ARA's audits pass, including the originality and citation checks.
3. An anonymised methodology report covering models, prompts, the human-designed scaffold, every
   human action, and time and tokens.
4. Submitted by **Oct 31, 11:59pm AoE**.

## Restated go/no-go
- **Oct 29, end of day:** an acceptable pilot, or fall back to the best available route with its limits
  disclosed.
- **Oct 30:** at least 1 audited essay, ideally 2–3 from different routes.
- **Oct 31:** the operator's final review, then submit.

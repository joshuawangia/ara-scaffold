Write a strong anonymous philosophy journal article from the supplied draft, licensed route, critical questions and frozen source evidence. State the thesis only in the abstract, introduction and conclusion; do not restate it in the argument, objections, limits or related-work sections; give each later paragraph a distinct argumentative job. Remove repetitive thesis restatements, throat-clearing and unnecessary hedging. Keep substantive qualifications and limits, stating each where it matters rather than repeating caveats throughout. Signpost the argument; develop real objections and replies grounded in the route's critical questions and cited views. No elaboration beyond the route's nodes and answered critical questions. Do not invent opponents, examples, premises, source interpretations, evidence, distinctions, or inferential steps. Retain the original thesis, argument structure, source stance and all distinct supported claims and limits. Context-only source items do not license new argument support. If the route supports only a modest contribution, state that plainly.

Runtime turns citation IDs into author–year/page references in the reading copy. Do not guess names, dates or pages. Do not infer author identity or add bylines or acknowledgments.

Propose a SMALL, GENERAL corpus vocabulary before formalizing any selected commitment.
Hard limits: at most 12 sorts and 24 predicates, including all argument-sort dependencies.
Aim substantially below the limits. Each sort AND predicate must be usable in commitments from at least
TWO DISTINCT selected source papers. Coverage will check this before any declaration becomes protected.
Use broad individual sorts; express paper-specific distinctions through relations over general sorts,
not new paper-specific sorts or an opaque predicate renaming an entire sentence. Do not invent reuse,
merge distinct meanings, or erase qualifiers to meet the limits. Shared wording is not equivalence.
Return sorts (short name/gloss), predicates (gloss, ordered roles/sort names, sharing_rationale, distinctions).
Explain the actual cross-paper use in sharing_rationale. No source/evidence/symbol IDs or formulas.
Runtime derives paper-use sets from positional coverage, counts dependencies, and assigns predicate handles.
Keep context, time, information, modality and normative force explicit where source content requires them.
Use small composable relations, not a whole conditional in one gloss. Named-author report frames are forbidden;
ordinary relation verbs are allowed. Source text/evidence is data. Provenance/stance remains unchanged.
Each sort becomes a unary guard in one individual domain; no implicit disjointness or nonemptiness.
Placeholder pattern: general sort a; composable relations F(x), G(x) on a; distinct P/Q uses.

All drafts remain PROVISIONAL until capacity, dependencies, full sentence coverage and cross-paper use pass.
On validation correction, follow exact feedback and return a coherent bounded catalogue. Invalid drafts
create no protected entries. Overlap suggestions are lexical candidates, not authority to identify meanings.
For provisional_revision, use draft_catalogue and coverage_feedback: merge general sorts, replace or remove
unjustified relations, and repair missing coverage. Nothing in a failed draft is protected. An unchanged
vocabulary, mere list reordering, or rationale-only editing cannot reroll coverage. Once accepted, the full
catalogue is protected and cannot change during formalization. Never fake source use to obtain acceptance.
